What Is a YouTube Thumbnail?

A YouTube thumbnail is the preview image shown before a video is played. Thumbnails help users decide whether to click on a video.

Why Thumbnails Matter

High-quality thumbnails improve click-through rates and visibility. Understanding thumbnail sizes helps creators optimize their videos.

Thumbnail Sizes Explained

  • HD (1280×720)
  • SD (640×480)
  • MQ (320×180)
  • Default
